# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Disagree about the work, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Critique code, decisions and ideas — not people.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A rejected contribution is not a judgement of you.
- Say when you are unsure. "I couldn't test this" is more useful than false confidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments related to who someone is.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits — and
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to **rishabhgupta4523@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and
reviewed as promptly as a small project allows.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate
this policy, and may ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Where a
warning is enough, a warning is what you get.
